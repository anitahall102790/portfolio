About Me

I've spent my career at the intersection of people, technology, and delivery.

I started my career as a software developer, but I quickly realized that the part of the work I loved most wasn't just building the solution. It was understanding the problem, bringing the right people together, and figuring out how to get from an idea to something that actually worked for the people using it.

That realization shaped the rest of my career. Over the last decade, I've moved from development into business analysis, technical delivery, project leadership, and increasingly, product thinking. I've worked across the full lifecycle of complex technical projects, translating business needs, partnering with engineers, navigating constraints, managing stakeholders, and helping teams turn ambiguity into a clear path forward.

What has stayed consistent is that I genuinely enjoy solving complicated problems with people. I care about the experience of the users we're building for, the teams doing the work, and creating enough clarity that everyone understands what we're trying to accomplish and why.

How I Work

Technical Translation

I bridge the gap between business needs and technical teams, turning complex problems into clear, actionable work while helping stakeholders understand technical decisions and constraints.

Delivery Leadership

I bring structure to complex work, connecting priorities, dependencies, stakeholders, and teams so everyone understands where we're going and what it will take to get there.

Product Thinking

I keep the problem and the people we're solving it for at the center of the work. I think beyond whether something can be delivered to whether we're building the right solution and creating meaningful value.

Technical Toolkit

I started my career in software development, and that technical foundation still shapes how I approach the work today. It helps me ask better questions, understand constraints, and work effectively with the teams building the solution.
